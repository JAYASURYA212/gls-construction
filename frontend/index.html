Our Construction Process

How we take your project from idea to reality.

01

Consultation

We meet to understand your requirement, budget, and vision for the project.

02

Site Visit

Our team visits and evaluates the construction site in Dharmapuri.

03

Planning

We analyze the requirements and prepare the initial project plan.

04

Design

Drafting of 2D floor plans and 3D elevations where required.

05

Estimation

We prepare approximate material quantities and the total cost estimate.

06

Quotation

You receive a clear, detailed, and transparent project quotation.

07

Construction

We begin earthwork, foundation, and manage the complete structural construction.

08

Finishing

Executing flooring, painting, electrical wiring, plumbing, and all finishing touches.

09

Inspection

A thorough final inspection is conducted to ensure quality and make any corrections.

10

Handover

We complete the project, hand over the keys, and you step into your new space.
